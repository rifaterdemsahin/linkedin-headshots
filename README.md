## Video Production Process

Pipeline for producing the "tell" sections of Skool AI Certification community promotional LinkedIn videos:

1. **Record** the raw take.
2. **Chroma key** the footage with [chroma-keyer](https://github.com/rifaterdemsahin/chroma-keyer) to make it Canva-ready.
3. **Cut silent portions** with [footage-cut-silent-portions](https://github.com/rifaterdemsahin/footage-cut-silent-portions).
4. **Add captions** and compose the final cut in Canva.
5. **Publish** to LinkedIn.

Estimated time: ~50–60 minutes per video.
