LinkedIn Video Production Process

An end-to-end pipeline for producing short, chroma-keyed promotional videos for the Skool AI Certification community — used for the "tell" sections of LinkedIn content.

1

Record

Record the raw take on camera/webcam — the "tell" segment for the LinkedIn promo, following the one-minute audience-connects pre-production plan.

~10–15 min per take (incl. retakes)
2

Chroma Key

Run the raw footage through chroma-keyer to strip the green screen and export a Canva-ready clip with a transparent/replaceable background.

~10 min
3

Cut Silence

Pass the keyed clip through footage-cut-silent-portions to automatically trim dead air and silent gaps, tightening the pacing.

~10 min
4

Captions & Compose in Canva

Bring the cut, keyed clip into Canva, layer captions/text and any brand elements, and finalize the composition.

~15–20 min
5

Publish to LinkedIn

Export the final video and publish it to LinkedIn as part of the Skool AI Certification community promotion.

~5 min
~50–60 minutes
estimated end-to-end time per video

Why this process

These videos are the "tell" sections of a hook–tell–ask style LinkedIn post, promoting the Skool AI Certification community. Keeping the pipeline scripted and repo-driven (chroma key → silence cut → Canva → publish) keeps each video's turnaround under an hour while maintaining a consistent, professional look.

Repos used in this pipeline: chroma-keyer · footage-cut-silent-portions
